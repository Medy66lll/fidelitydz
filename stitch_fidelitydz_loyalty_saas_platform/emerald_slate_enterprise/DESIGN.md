## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered specifically for high-stakes operational oversight, sovereign enterprise governance, and bilingual multi-tenant loyalty network management. It balances the institutional gravitas of a deep midnight chassis with the vitality of Algerian emerald green, projecting unwavering security, fiscal precision, and modern technological stewardship.

### Visual Style: Architectural Modern Corporate
The system merges crisp, low-contrast structural minimalism with hybrid tonal layering. Deep slate navigation and structural anchors provide an executive, high-contrast framing around high-density, ultra-clean data canvases. Surfaces prioritize low optical noise, micro-fine boundaries (1px subtle rules), and strategic warm amber highlights to denote institutional tiering, alerts, and transaction statuses without inducing operator fatigue during prolonged administrative sessions.

### Bilingual Architectural Tenet
Designed from inception for French/Latin and Arabic dual-script administration. Layouts honor bidirectional parity (LTR/RTL), matching visual weight, vertical baseline harmony, and optical density across script boundaries without breaking component geometry.

## Colors

The palette uses an executive dual-environment structure: high-contrast dark chassis elements (navigation bar, master shell, drawer headers) paired with an ultra-legible, daylight-optimized administrative canvas for heavy tabular data.

## Typography

The type scale prioritizes numerical scanability, tabular alignment, and strict bi-script parity. **Plus Jakarta Sans** governs the Latin and French administrative infrastructure, chosen for its contemporary geometric balance, open counters, and legible figure set.

### Arabic Dual-Script Rules (Cairo Pairing)
When the interface switches to Arabic (RTL) or renders bilingual badges/tooltips:
- Substitute the font family dynamically with **Cairo**.
- Because Arabic typography renders visually smaller than Latin at identical pixel dimensions, scale Cairo upward by `+1px` for all body and label tokens under `16px`.
- Maintain standard line heights to preserve rigid table cell heights across language switches.
- Numerical displays across both French and Arabic remain tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for currency (DZD), point accounts, and serial transaction logs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a rigid, structured desktop-first admin console pattern with full fluid-responsive reflow capabilities.

### Shell Architecture
- **Persistent Global Sidebar:** Fixed 260px desktop width (`#0F172A`). Collapses into a 72px icon-only rail on screens below `1280px`, transitioning to an off-canvas drawer overlay on mobile (`<768px`).
- **Main Viewport Canvas:** Fluid grid with fixed minimum padding (`margin: 2rem`), adapting downward to `margin-sm: 1rem` on handheld screens.
- **Split-Screen Inspection Pane:** When inspecting ledger lines, tenant accounts, or system audits, an inline contextual drawer slides in over a 480px fixed width, compressing the tabular canvas fluidly.

### Grid & Component Rhythm
A standard 12-column layout grid operates across screens with a `1.5rem` (`24px`) base gutter. All component gaps, internal paddings, and card layouts strictly observe an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid:
- Form fields and button interiors leverage `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) vertical metrics with `space-md` (16px) lateral insets.
- KPI metric cards utilize `space-lg` (24px) internal containment to ensure data breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, purposeful contrast rather than excessive drop shadows. The design system uses low-contrast crisp borders combined with ambient slate shadows to define spatial separation.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`, zero elevation, completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Cards, KPI Blocks, Table Rows):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Select Menus, Filter Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Inspection Side Drawer, Modal Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Shadow: `-10px 0 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), -4px 0 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)` (inverted for RTL Arabic viewports).
- **Dark Tier Containers (Sidebar, High-Tier Terminal Badges):** `#1E293B` nested within `#0F172A`, framed by `1px solid #334155`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric precision through balanced corner radii (`roundedness: 2`). This avoids the toy-like appearance of fully rounded components while eliminating the sterile harshness of raw rectangles.

### Radius Assignments
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Data Cards, KPI Stat Blocks, Modal Shells:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` (`12px` to `16px`).
- **Status Pills, Verification Badges, Avatar Indicators:** Full pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately signal categorical and dynamic status indicators at glance speed.
- **Table Cells & Row Highlights:** Subtle inner corner radii (`4px`) on hover bounding boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#10B981` background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, subtle inner-bevel highlight. Hover transitions smoothly to `#059669`. Active state deepens to `#047857`. Focus ring: `2px offset 2px solid #10B981`.
- **Secondary (Subtle Slate):** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive:** Solid `#EF4444` or subtle tint `#FEF2F2` with `#DC2626` text for secondary risk actions.
- **Chassis Actions (Sidebar):** Translucent emerald (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`) background on `#0F172A` with `#10B981` text for active navigational triggers.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0 12px`, text color `#0F172A`. Focused state: border `#10B981`, ring `3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- **Search Bar with Omnisearch:** Integrated shortcut badge (`⌘K`), magnifying icon left-aligned (LTR) or right-aligned (RTL).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Emerald filled (`#10B981`) when checked, with crisp white iconography. Unchecked state: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.

### Data Tables (High-Density Back-Office Standard)
- **Header:** `#F8FAFC` background, `border-bottom: 1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `label-md` (`#475569`, uppercase, tracking `0.05em`).
- **Rows:** White background, zebra hover `#F8FAFC`, row height fixed `52px` (standard) or `40px` (dense audit mode). Separated by `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- **Monetary/Points Alignment:** Always right-aligned in LTR (left-aligned in RTL), using tabular lining figures.

### Status Badges & Verification Indicators
- **Verified / Active Merchant:** Pill shape, `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text, leading `6px` solid `#10B981` indicator dot.
- **Pending Audit / Warning:** Pill shape, `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text.
- **Suspended / High-Risk:** Pill shape, `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text.
- **Super-Admin Sovereign Tag:** Deep Slate `#0F172A` background, `#F59E0B` (Amber) border and typography, featuring a micro-star glyph.

### KPI Stat Cards
- Enclosed in white card shells (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), padding `1.5rem`.
- Layout: Top line with label and category icon; primary data row displaying large tabular numerical metrics (`headline-lg`); bottom trend indicator showing green (`+X%`) or red (`-X%`) micro-chips accompanied by comparative time-frame text.

### Inspection Side Drawer
- Docked to right screen boundary (docked left in Arabic viewports).
- Header framed in `#0F172A` with emerald accent indicator for immediate visual context switch.
- Tabbed sub-navigation for quick toggling: "Profile Details", "Transaction Ledger", "Security & Keys", and "Audit Log".
